Mathterpiece

A language for math you can touch.

  1. Declare what the figure is, does, and looks like.
  2. The compiler typechecks every possible state.
  3. The runtime makes it accessible and interactive.

 1; chords in a circle: from each point k to the point m × k
 2(int n 200 (const))
 3(real m 1 (>= 1) (<= 12))  ; typechecked: m stays in 1 to 12
 4(func spot ((real k)) (polar 1 (turn (/ k n))))
 5
 6; what the figure is
 7(scene chords
 8  (circle rim (r 1) (fill none) (stroke 'gray'))
 9  (foreach k n
10    (line_ (from (spot k)) (to (spot (* m k)))
11           ; what the figure looks like
12           (stroke (mix 'crimson' 'royalblue' (/ k n)))
13           (stroke-opacity 0.5))))

15; what it does
16(animation sweep (loop)
17  (move m (from 1) (to 12) (duration 40))
18  (move m (from 12) (to 1) (duration 40)))
19(canvas v (x -1.1) (y -1.1) (width 2.2) (height 2.2) chords
20  (on-ready (animation sweep)))

Use cases

One source makes a figure for each place where math is explained.

  • PapersA figure in a preprint that readers can explore, next to the claim that it shows.
  • SlidesA figure that you drag during a talk, in place of a series of still frames.
  • Lecture notesA figure that students try for themselves, with the course text around it.
  • DigestionsA page that works through someone else's result, with a figure at each step.
  • VideosA figure's animations, played and recorded from the same source as the page.

The compiler checks every figure

The compiler works out the range of every value. When a value could leave the range that a shape needs, the figure does not compile, and the error gives the line, the column and a way to fix it.

circle.mtp
(real k 1 (>= 0) (<= 3))
(slider size (move k (from 0) (to 3)))
(scene s (circle c (r (- k 2))))
mtp check circle.mtp
circle.mtp:3:23: c's r may break (>= 0): constrain what it reads so this can be proved, or declare c as circle|none

A figure that could draw something undefined, such as a circle with a negative radius, never reaches a reader.

Every figure gets the same care

These come with the runtime, so no figure has to build them again.

  • KeyboardSliders, buttons and players work from the keyboard.
  • Screen readersEach control and each canvas carries a label written in the source.
  • TouchEvery drag also works by tapping.
  • Light and darkEach color has a dark value, and the page picks.
  • Reduced motionAnimations respect the reader's setting.
  • Saved as dataA figure is a .mtpc file, so it keeps working when the runtime improves.
