About Mathterpiece

Mathterpiece (mtp) is a language for math figures that readers can drag, slide and play, on any web page. You declare a figure in a short source file. The compiler checks every value that the figure can take, and a runtime draws it with touch, keyboard and screen reader support.

Open source

The compiler and the runtime are open source, under the MIT license. The source, the issues and the history are on GitHub.

Status

The language is still changing. Its specification is written at spec.mtp-lang.org, and the compiler and the runtime will be published to PyPI and npm when version 1 of the specification is done. Until then, the quickstart installs Mathterpiece from the repository.

Questions and contact

Bug reports go to the issues on GitHub. For anything else, write to hello@aris.pub.

Who makes it

Mathterpiece is made by Leo Torres, and it is part of The Aris Program.
